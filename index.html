HOW TO PLAY
🐴
MOVE
Joystick or WASD / Arrow keys to move your horse
🎮
CAMERA
Drag the screen to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom
⚡
GALLOP
Tap GALLOP button or hold Shift for full speed
🔀
MOUNT / DISMOUNT
Tap ACTION / press E to mount or dismount your horse
🏹
ARCHERY
Hold AIM / F to draw the bow. Release to fire
🌿
FORAGE
Press G near glowing plants to collect herbs & berries
🌅
SEASONS
Press SEASON button or K to cycle through all four seasons
🔭
EXPLORE
Climb the watchtower, ride the ropeway & spot wildlife
🌟 Explore the trail — discover hidden landmarks, fish at the pond, sit by a fire & name constellations at night!
